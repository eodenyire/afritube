## 🚨 CRITICAL GAPS (Must Fix First)

### 1. Monetization (0% - CRITICAL)
**Why:** Can't pay creators → No creator incentive → Platform dies
- Ad serving system
- Revenue share
- Payout infrastructure
- Payment processing
- Tax compliance

**Impact:** Without this, creators have no reason to stay

---

### 2. Recommendation Engine (0% - CRITICAL)
**Why:** No personalized discovery → Users see random content → Low engagement
- Personalized home feed
- Up next suggestions
- Search ranking
- Trending page
- ML models

**Impact:** YouTube's core product - drives 80% of watch time

---

### 3. Livestreaming (0% - CRITICAL)
**Why:** Missing major engagement surface → Lower retention
- RTMP ingest
- Live chat
- Superchat (monetization)
- Stream scheduling
- Real-time infrastructure

**Impact:** 30% of YouTube watch time is live

---

### 4. Shorts System (0% - CRITICAL)
**Why:** Missing TikTok-style engagement loop → Can't compete with TikTok
- Vertical video
- Infinite scroll
- Music library
- Duets/remixes
- Effects

**Impact:** Shorts drives 25% of YouTube engagement

---

### 5. Rights Management (0% - CRITICAL)
**Why:** Copyright liability → Legal risk → Platform shutdown
- Audio fingerprinting
- Video fingerprinting
- Claims workflow
- Takedown process
- Revenue sharing

**Impact:** YouTube's Content ID is worth billions

---

### 6. Moderation & Safety (0% - CRITICAL)
**Why:** Unmoderated content → Platform liability → Advertiser exodus
- Violence/nudity detection
- Hate speech detection
- Spam detection
- Human review queues
- Creator strikes

**Impact:** Advertisers won't run ads on unsafe platforms

---

### 7. Localization (0% - CRITICAL)
**Why:** English-only → Can't serve African markets → Miss your core audience
- Multi-language UI
- Multi-currency
- Regional content
- Local languages (Swahili, Yoruba, Hausa, etc.)

**Impact:** Africa is your competitive advantage

---

### 8. Africa-Specific Features (0% - CRITICAL)
**Why:** No local payment methods → Can't monetize African creators
- M-Pesa integration
- Airtel Money integration
- MTN MoMo integration
- Low-bandwidth mode
- Local creator discovery

**Impact:** This is what makes AfriTube different from YouTube
